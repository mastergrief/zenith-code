## Zone-split A/B/C

Known-zone = problems 1-20 (deterministic carry-over from N=20).
Novel-zone = problems 21-50 (first-time signal at this scale).

| Method | Known (1-20) | Novel (21-50) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| stock | 9/60 = 15.00% | 0/90 = 0.00% | 9/150 = 6.00% |
| dt-bias | 15/60 = 25.00% | 23/90 = 25.56% | 38/150 = 25.33% |
| **rename** | **17/60 = 28.33%** | **36/90 = 40.00%** | **53/150 = 35.33%** |

- Δ rename-vs-stock: **+44 cells (+29.33pp)**.
- Δ rename-vs-dt: **+15 cells (+9.99pp)** total; **+13 cells
  (+14.4pp)** novel-only.

Novel-only gap is the load-bearing signal: known-zone reproduces
prior N=20 deterministic, novel-zone is the generalization test
both methods see for the first time.

## DT-only interpretation

The 4 DT-only wins are real but weak — body-trajectory side signal
from first-token bias affecting subsequent decode, independent of
the def-header rename. Not globally null; carries one 3-cell
regression that RENAME does not.

For MBPP shipping: DT remains obsolete vs deterministic RENAME.
For future work: park as "body-bias may matter in a different task
class or stacked RENAME+DT experiment, must be re-tested with
regression gate." Not pursuing the stacking slice unless commercial
signal demands.
